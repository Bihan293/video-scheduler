Information is used to process video files, manage a queue, troubleshoot errors, and provide features you enable. Information is not intentionally sold. Third-party integrations may receive information needed to perform the requested function.

Storage and security

Local files and logs may remain on the device running the script. Delete them from your device when no longer needed. Protect API secrets and authorization tokens; never publish them in a repository.
